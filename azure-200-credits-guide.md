# How to Get $200 Free Azure Credits | Step-by-Step Guide 2026

Microsoft Azure provides $200 in free credits for new users for 30 days.  
This guide explains the exact process to claim them.

## Requirements
- Valid Email Address
- Phone Number
- Credit/Debit Card for identity verification. No charges will be made.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Go to Azure Free Page
Visit the official page: https://azure.microsoft.com/free

### Step 2: Sign Up
Click "Start Free" and sign in with your Microsoft account.

### Step 3: Verify Your Identity
Enter your phone number and card details. A $1 authorization may appear but will be refunded.

### Step 4: Start Using Credits
Your $200 credits will be activated immediately. They expire after 30 days.

## Important Tips
1. Set up budget alerts to track spending
2. Delete unused resources to save credits
3. Set spending limit to $0 to prevent charges after credits end
